# ADR-0100: A venue becoming unavailable flags a hold too, a replacement is always another venue, and the coordinator is told when the venue is available again

- **Status:** Accepted for flagging and releasing a hold, refusing a same-venue replacement for a venue that became unavailable and the "available again" notice (SCRUM-92 AC1, AC6, AC7, AC8, the Product Owner's decisions of 19 Oct); leaving a change-flagged arrangement's replacement free to stay at its venue (decision 5) follows the Product Owner's answers of 25 Oct, read together with the 19 Oct decision; sending the notice only when the last unavailability flag clears (decision 7) is a default to review, and the Product Owner confirmed it on 25 Oct; the notice's wording (decision 8) and one notice per arrangement are superseded by [ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md), which also locks a replacement for an unavailable venue to the flagged booking's period and, since the Product Owner's decisions of 26 Oct, tells the organiser of an event a venue sent back from Confirmed when the venue is available again or a replacement is approved
- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Story:** SCRUM-92 Flag bookings affected when a venue becomes unavailable ("Changed after review", 19 Oct)
- **Extends:** [ADR-0053](0053-flag-bookings-when-a-venue-becomes-unavailable.md), [ADR-0088](0088-replacing-a-flagged-venue-in-the-web-app.md); leaves [ADR-0094](0094-reassess-each-venue-arrangement-after-a-significant-change.md) decision 2 as it is

## Context
SCRUM-92 was In Review when the Product Owner settled four questions on 19 Oct and the criteria changed:

- "A tentative hold is a booking too, so an On Hold request is flagged" (AC1), and "if the flagged booking was On Hold, its hold is released" when a replacement is approved (AC7).
- "A replacement is always a different venue for the event's original period. Moving the event to another time is a change to the event, not a replacement" (AC6).
- When unavailability is removed, nothing happens automatically, but the assigned coordinator "is notified that the venue is available again" (AC8), whether a block was removed or the venue reactivated.

ADR-0053 had deferred holds until SCRUM-75 merged, which it since has. ADR-0053 flagged Pending, Conflicting and Approved arrangements through `FLAGGABLE_REQUEST_STATUSES`, and SCRUM-33's change reassessment (ADR-0094 decision 2) later read the same constant, deliberately leaving On Hold out of what a significant change flags. ADR-0053 decision 9 sent no notice when a flag cleared, and the replacement check (`_check_replaceable`) never compared venues.

## Decision
1. **A venue becoming unavailable has its own set of statuses.** `UNAVAILABILITY_FLAGGABLE_STATUSES` is Pending, Conflicting, On Hold and Approved. A block (`_arrangements_held_during`), a deactivation (`_arrangements_not_ended`), `needs_alternative_venue` and the release of a replaced arrangement read it. `FLAGGABLE_REQUEST_STATUSES` stays Pending, Conflicting and Approved and is now only what a significant change flags (`_live_arrangements_of`, `is_flagged_after_change`), so SCRUM-33's behaviour does not change.
2. **ADR-0094's On Hold default still stands.** A significant change still doesn't flag an arrangement On Hold, and `is_flagged_after_change` is still false for one. That waits for SCRUM-109 ([ADR-0129](0129-reassess-venue-bookings-after-an-approved-change-request.md) decision 10 says what can happen until then); nothing in SCRUM-92's new criteria says what a change should do to a hold.
3. **A hold is flagged by status, as the other statuses are.** A request On Hold whose expiry has passed but which SCRUM-94's job has not yet marked Expired is flagged too: the job turns it Expired, and an Expired arrangement no longer needs anything.
4. **Approving a replacement releases a flagged hold the way rejecting an event does** (ADR-0093): `_release_replaced` calls `_release_and_free_venue`, which cancels the hold linked to the request (or, for an ordinary approval, the booking found by its period) and marks the request Released. An original that stopped holding or asking for its venue before the replacement was approved, say rejected, is left as it is (ADR-0053 decision 11).
5. **A replacement at the unavailable venue it replaces is refused with a `400` on `venue_id`:** "A replacement must be at a different venue from the one it replaces." It is checked after the existing checks on `replaces_request_id` (not the event's, not flagged), whatever the period asked for (since [ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md), a different period at another venue is then refused on the time fields), and only while the replaced arrangement needs an alternative venue (`needs_alternative_venue`), so one flagged both ways is refused too. **An arrangement flagged only after a change keeps its rule:** a replacement at its own venue for a period that doesn't overlap the original is accepted, and approving one that overlaps it is refused as a clash, because the original's booking holds the venue until the replacement is approved (ADR-0045, ADR-0094 "Not covered here"). This follows the Product Owner's answers of 25 Oct: 6.4, "yes, clear a flag by raising a replacement request", and 6.1, flag every arrangement on a time change. A time change flags every arrangement, and the coordinator clears each flag with a replacement, so the event's own venue at its new time has to be allowed. The 19 Oct decision that "a replacement is always a different venue for the event's original period" was made about a venue becoming unavailable, where the same venue can't be used. Applying it only there is our reading of the two answers together. The web app still offers only other venues from its search (ADR-0088 decision 4); the coordinator can type the same venue's ID into the form.
6. **The Safety Review gate and confirmation name a flagged hold twice,** as they do a flagged Pending request: once because it is not Approved ("Venue arrangement 12 is on_hold.") and once because it needs an alternative venue. `_venue_gaps` needed no change: it reads `list_unapproved_arrangements` and `needs_alternative_venue`.
7. **AC8: the assigned coordinator is told when the venue is available again** (and, since [ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md) decision 10, the organiser of an event a venue sent back), with a new `venue_available_again` notification kind (migration 0044). Removing a block, or reactivating a venue, sends one notice for each live arrangement at the venue that the block, or the deactivation, had flagged and that is left with no other unavailability flag. **Default to review:** an arrangement another block still covers gets no notice, because its venue is not available again for it; it gets one when the last flag clears. The notice is worked out before the flags are deleted, sent after the commit (ADR-0015), goes to the coordinator assigned now, and is lost alone, and logged, if it can't be saved. An arrangement already released by its replacement gets none, since it no longer holds or asks for its venue.
8. **Superseded by [ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md) decisions 6 and 7** (the Product Owner's wording of 25 Oct, one notice per event). **The notice reads as the flag's notice does.** Its message is "A venue that was unavailable for your event is available again. Its original arrangement stands." and its `detail` names the venue, the arrangement and its period in business time: `Main Hall is available again for Main hall (2026-11-01 09:00 to 2026-11-01 12:00).` It links to the event, as every notice does, and names the event through the notification's own `event_name`. Nothing else changes: a Confirmed event sent back to Approved stays Approved (ADR-0053 decision 8), and a replacement already raised stays Pending (ADR-0053 decision 12).
9. **The mock backend mirrors the backend.** The booking mock flags On Hold (the held event's studio is flagged in its fixtures), keeps `needs_alternative_venue` only while an arrangement is live, releases a flagged hold and clears its expiry when its replacement is approved, leaves an original that stopped holding its venue as it is, and refuses a same-venue replacement on `venue_id` while the original needs an alternative venue, accepting one for an arrangement flagged only after a change. The notifications mock only serves seeded notices, so it raises no "available again" notice; the integration test checks the real one. The venue mocks still don't flag the booking mock's arrangements when a venue is blocked or deactivated: the booking mock's flags come from its fixtures (ADR-0088 decision 7).

## Consequences
- The web app needed no change: it already shows `needs_alternative_venue` whatever the status (ADR-0088 decision 2), already shows a field error under "Venue ID" in the replacement form, and its notification list shows the backend's message and detail as sent.
- A coordinator may now hear about one flagged arrangement up to three times: flagged, available again, and the booking decision on its replacement.
- `notification_kind` gains a value, so the downgrade rebuilds the type and deletes the new rows, as 0041's does.

## Revisit when
- SCRUM-109 decides what a change to the event's date or time does to a hold.
- SCRUM-59 lets Venue Staff reconfirm a booking: decide whether a change-flagged arrangement is still replaced at its own venue, or reconfirmed in place.
- ~~The customer wants a notice when one of several blocks over an arrangement is removed.~~ The Product Owner decided on 25 Oct that it waits until nothing else covers the booking ([ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md)).
- The venue mocks should flag and clear the booking mock's arrangements when a venue is blocked, deactivated, unblocked or reactivated.
