# ADR-0099: Once the event is confirmed, its organiser's record lists every approved venue arrangement and shows the Safety Officer's final decision

- **Status:** Accepted for showing the confirmed event's venues, date, times and arrangements to its organiser (SCRUM-49 AC11) and the Safety Officer's final decision with when it was made (the Product Owner's decision of 25 Oct); showing none of it before the event is Confirmed, still showing it once it is Completed, and which details of an arrangement are shown are defaults pending the Product Owner; keeping the officer's name, the checklist notes and the reason hidden is a default to review
- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Stories:** SCRUM-49 AC11
- **Supersedes:** [ADR-0051](0051-confirm-an-event.md) decision 10 (the organiser's record had no venues, and the venue module no organiser-facing read)
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0098](0098-organiser-sees-their-equipment-without-internal-notes.md) (the organiser's own record and its body); the staff record's ADR-0095, on its own branch, for the current safety check's outcome

## Context
SCRUM-49 AC11: "Given the event is confirmed, Then every venue, the date, time and confirmed arrangements are visible to the Event Organiser." ADR-0051 decision 10 left this out: the organiser's record (`GET /api/events/requests/<reference>`) carried the event's own date and times but no venues, and the only read of an event's arrangements, `GET /api/venues/events/<reference>/booking-requests`, is the coordinator's. That list carries every status, Venue Staff's decision reason, the preparation status and notes, the justification and the ids.

ADR-0051 question 2 asked the customer what the organiser should see once the event is confirmed, whether that includes the Safety Officer's decision, and whether they see anything before. The AC answers the venues. The Product Owner answered the Safety Officer's part on 25 Oct: "show the Safety Officer's final decision on the confirmed event" to the Event Organiser. Whether the organiser sees anything before confirmation is still open.

## Decision
1. **A new venue module read, `list_organiser_venue_arrangements(event_id)`,** in its own section at the end of `venue/services.py`. It returns the event's **approved** arrangements, earliest period first (the order of the event's own list), each as an `OrganiserVenueArrangement`: the venue's name and location, the period (`starts_at`, `ends_at`), the purpose, the layout and the expected occupancy. The events module calls it through the venue module's services, never its models (CLAUDE.md's module boundaries).
2. **Only approved arrangements.** Confirmation needs every active arrangement Approved (ADR-0051), so on a confirmed event these are its confirmed arrangements. A Pending replacement, a Rejected, On Hold, Conflicting or Expired one, and a Withdrawn or Released one are left out, since none of them holds a venue for the event.
3. **Nothing internal.** Venue Staff's decision reason, the preparation status and notes, the justification for an unsuitable venue, the reassessment and alternative-venue flags, and every id stay out (`OrganiserVenueArrangementSchema`).
4. **Default pending the Product Owner: when the organiser sees them.** The record lists the venues, and shows the safety decision, once the event is **Confirmed**, and still once it is **Completed**, since what it was held at and approved for doesn't stop being true (`CONFIRMED_DETAILS_STATUSES`). In every other status the list is empty and the decision `null`, even when some arrangements are already approved or the Safety Officer has ruled: the AC's literal reading ("once confirmed"). The alternative, showing them while the event is planned, stays ADR-0051 question 2's open part. A confirmed event that a significant change sends back to Approved (ADR-0087, ADR-0094) hides them again until it is confirmed again, and a cancelled or rejected event shows none.
5. **Default pending the Product Owner: what an arrangement shows.** "The confirmed arrangements" is read as what was booked: the venue and where it is, when, and the purpose, layout and expected occupancy the coordinator asked for. The venue's capacity, facilities and features are not shown; the organiser can ask the coordinator.
6. **The record's body.** The organiser's record gains `venue_arrangements`, a list (empty until the event is confirmed), and `safety_decision` (decision 10), beside ADR-0098's organiser equipment lines: `OrganiserEventRecord`, dumped by the route's `_organiser_event_record_body`. Times are business time with an offset (ADR-0009). The coordinator's record has neither key: they see every arrangement, in every status, on the venue requests page, and follow the check through its notices and the history.
7. **The organiser's page shows a "Venues" section** (`OrganiserVenues`) once the list has anything in it: a table captioned "Venues booked" with the columns Venue (name, then location), When (the date and the time range, as the Safety Officer's card writes them), Purpose, Layout (its label, or "No layout set", as a booking request's details say it) and Expected occupancy.
8. **The mock backend does the same** (CONVENTIONS §14): the record mock reads the approved arrangements from the booking mock's store and the venue names from the venue mock, and adds them to the organiser's record only once the event is Confirmed or Completed. A confirmed Harbour Gala (`EVR-BOOK0112`, in Sara's organisation, coordinated by Marcus, who is no mock account) holds two approved venues, saved out of period order, with Venue Staff's reasons and preparation the organiser must not see, and a withdrawn one. Venue Staff see it like any event with bookings, so their lists' tests count it.
9. **Tests.** The venue read is tested for every status (built from the enum) and for another event's arrangements; the organiser's record for Confirmed and Completed, and for every other status (built from the enum), for both the venues and the safety decision; the route for the body and its business times, and for the coordinator's record having neither key. The safety review journey (`tests/e2e`) signs in the organiser and reads their record before and after confirmation. The integration test (`organiserConfirmedEvent.int.test.ts`) confirms a real event and reads it as the organiser before and after, and as the coordinator; it needs a Lead, Venue Staff and a Safety Officer, so it skips without them, as the other confirmation checks do.
10. **The Product Owner's decision of 25 Oct: the Safety Officer's final decision.** `safety_decision` is `{"outcome", "decided_at"}` (`OrganiserSafetyDecisionSchema`, dumped from `OrganiserSafetyDecision`): the outcome of the event's **current** safety check and when it was recorded, or `null`. "Final" is the current check's outcome as `_current_safety_outcome_entry` reads it (ADR-0047: the newest entry that opens, settles or withdraws a check decides), so an earlier "changes requested" on a check the event later passed is not shown. On a confirmed event that is the approval, since confirmation needs one and a withdrawal sends the event back to Approved; if the current check has no outcome on record, the decision is `null`.
    - The helper is the staff record's (ADR-0095): this branch carries that branch's `refactor(SCRUM-24): find the outcome of the current safety check in one helper` commit unchanged, so rebasing over it drops the copy as already applied. The organiser's own function and type are `_safety_decision_for_organiser` and `OrganiserSafetyDecision`, beside the organiser's record, which moves below the safety check helpers so each helper sits above its caller.
11. **Default to review: the officer's name, the checklist notes and the reason stay hidden.** The officer's name is left out as the equipment reviewer's is (SCRUM-48's organiser visibility clarification). The checklist notes are the officer's internal review material. The reason, which holds any condition of an approval (for example "keep the terrace exit clear"), goes to the coordinator in the outcome's notice, and the coordinator passes on what the organiser needs, as SCRUM-48 has them do for the outcome note. There is no separate conditions field.
12. **The organiser's page shows a "Safety check" section** once there is a decision: the outcome in words ("Approved") and when it was decided ("Decided 4 Oct, 2:00 pm"), never who.
13. **The mock reads the decision from its event history,** as the backend does, through the history store, which moves to `eventHistoryStore.ts` so the record mock can read it without importing the history route's module (which imports the record mock). `EVR-BOOK0102`, confirmed, carries the Safety Officer's approval of 4 Oct; `EVR-W9CR5E7M`, approved with a "changes requested" outcome, shows none; the confirmed gala, with no check on record, shows `null`. The Safety Officer mock does not write history entries yet, so an outcome recorded through it doesn't reach this decision; that gap is the history mock's, listed as a ticket.

## Consequences
- The organiser can see where and when their confirmed event takes place, and that it passed its safety check, without asking the coordinator.
- Opening the organiser's record of a confirmed event reads its arrangements and their venues, and its newest safety-check entry.
- If the Product Owner wants these shown while the event is planned, only `CONFIRMED_DETAILS_STATUSES` (and the mock's copy) and the tests change.

## Revisit when
- The Product Owner answers ADR-0051 question 2's open part: whether the organiser sees the arrangements or the safety check before the event is confirmed, and whether they should see more of each venue or of the officer's decision.
- A change request (SCRUM-56, SCRUM-57) changes a confirmed event's arrangements: check what the organiser sees while it is reviewed.
