# ADR-0104: The organiser sees everything on their event's record, at every stage

- **Status:** Accepted. What the organiser sees is the Product Owner's decision of 25 Oct, extended on 26 Oct to the requested venues' capacity, facilities and accessibility features and the equipment shortfall (SCRUM-24 AC10, SCRUM-49 AC13, SCRUM-37 AC5, SCRUM-48 AC5, SCRUM-84 AC2, SCRUM-85 AC2, SCRUM-33 AC1). The Product Owner also confirmed on 26 Oct that the safety section shows only the current check (decision 4) and that the organiser reads every kind of history entry (decision 5). How it is built (the organiser's record carrying the staff record's sections), keeping the `internal_planning_notes` key in the API, and the page's wording are defaults to review
- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Stories:** SCRUM-24 AC10; SCRUM-49 AC13; SCRUM-37 AC5; SCRUM-48 AC5; SCRUM-84 AC2; SCRUM-85 AC2; SCRUM-33 AC1, AC5; SCRUM-93 AC5
- **Supersedes:** the hiding rules of [ADR-0098](0098-organiser-sees-their-equipment-without-internal-notes.md) (decisions 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9) and [ADR-0099](0099-organiser-sees-a-confirmed-event.md) (decisions 2-5, 10-12); [ADR-0025](0025-event-edits-significant-changes-and-history.md)'s internal-only history fields and the internal-only history kinds of ADR-0037, ADR-0047, ADR-0049, ADR-0050 and ADR-0053
- **Builds on:** [ADR-0095](0095-read-only-event-record-for-internal-staff.md) (the staff record's sections)

## Context
On 25 Oct the Product Owner decided that the Event Organiser sees everything on their own organisation's event records, read-only, with no exceptions. SCRUM-24 AC10 now reads: "An Event Organiser can open any submitted event belonging to their own client organisation and sees everything on its record, read-only, at every stage, not only once it is Confirmed: the event details and planning notes, every venue arrangement with its status (including holds with their expiry and flags with their reason), the equipment lines with their technical notes, requested and provided quantities, outcomes, notes and reviewer, the Operational Safety Check outcome with its date, checklist and comments, preparation details, the clarification record, the change history and the names of the staff who acted. Nothing on the event record is hidden from them. They cannot see other client organisations' events, another user's drafts (SCRUM-20) or the venue availability calendar (SCRUM-26)." SCRUM-49 AC13 adds that nothing is hidden when a Confirmed event returns to Approved. SCRUM-37, SCRUM-48, SCRUM-84 and SCRUM-85 now name the organiser among the readers, and SCRUM-33 AC1 renames "internal planning notes" to "planning notes".

Before this, the organiser's record (`GET /api/events/requests/<reference>`) sent a reduced view of each equipment line (ADR-0098), the approved venues and the safety outcome only once the event was Confirmed (ADR-0099), and no planning notes, reservations or preparation. The history route hid nine kinds of entry and any change to the planning notes from organisers.

## Decision
1. **The organiser's record carries the staff record's sections**, in place of keeping a parallel organiser reader for each. `get_event_record_for_organiser` returns an `OrganiserEventRecord`: the sections every record shows (`EventRecord`), then every venue arrangement, the equipment lines, the reservations and the current safety outcome. The reservations and the safety outcome reuse the staff record's readers and schemas unchanged (`list_reservations_of_event`, `_safety_outcome_of`, `StaffReservationSchema`, `SafetyOutcomeSchema`). The route adds them, and the planning notes, to the body every record has (`_organiser_event_record_body`, `OrganiserRecordSectionsSchema`). Nothing waits on the event's status. *Default to review: the shape.*
2. **Every arrangement, in full.** The venue module's `list_every_arrangement_for_record` lists every arrangement in any status (withdrawn, released, rejected and expired too), earliest period first. Each `ArrangementForRecord` now holds everything on the arrangement but its ids, under the coordinator's own list's names: the venue's name and location, the period, the purpose, the expected occupancy, the layout, the justification, the status, Venue Staff's decision reason, the hold's expiry (`hold_expires_at`, shown while On Hold or once Expired, read from one model property the coordinator's list now shares), each unavailability flag with its category, note and period, each reassessment flag with its kind and reason, and the preparation (`RecordVenueArrangementSchema`). *Default to review: these are "every venue field".*
    - **The venue's capacity, facilities and accessibility features** (`venue_capacity`, `venue_facilities`, `venue_accessibility_features`) are on each arrangement too: the Product Owner's extension of 26 Oct, since they are what the Safety Officer reviews (SCRUM-97 AC6) and what the organiser's own accessibility needs are checked against. They come from the venue module's own reader, so the events module still reads venues only through venue services. They are on every arrangement, whatever its status, since each one is a venue "requested for the event" (a decision we took: the AC doesn't say whether a withdrawn request still counts). The venue's supported layouts are not added: the AC names only these three. This does not open the venue catalogue (`GET /api/venues/...`) or venue search to the organiser; those routes keep their roles.
3. **Each equipment line in full.** `EquipmentLineOnRecord` (`RecordEquipmentLineSchema`, the Safety Officer's line plus three fields) has the type by name, the quantity, the technical notes, the status, the quantity provided, the shortfall, the outcome note, and who reviewed it and when, by name.
4. **The safety check is the current one's**, as on the staff record (ADR-0095 decision 5): its outcome, checklist, reason, officer and time. Once an approval is withdrawn, or the event is sent for a new check, the section shows no outcome until the next one; the earlier outcomes and the withdrawal are in the history, which the organiser now reads in full (decision 5). *Agreed: the Product Owner confirmed this on 26 Oct.*
5. **The history is the same for the organiser as for staff.** `INTERNAL_ONLY_KINDS`, `INTERNAL_ONLY_FIELDS` and `_shown_to_organiser` are gone: the organiser sees reservations, equipment and safety outcomes, preparation updates, a venue becoming unavailable, a withdrawn approval and changes to the planning notes, each with the name of who acted. Their scope is unchanged: their own organisation's events only. *Agreed: the Product Owner confirmed every kind on 26 Oct.*
6. **Nothing is hidden after a Confirmed event returns to Approved.** The record never looked at the status, so the arrangements stay listed with their current status (SCRUM-49 AC13).
7. **"Planning notes" wherever users see them.** The edit form's field, the staff record's section and its empty message, and the history's "changed" line say "Planning notes", and the edit form no longer says only staff see them. *Default to review:* the API keeps the `internal_planning_notes` key, which no user sees, so no client or migration changes.
8. **The page.** The organiser's event page shows "Planning notes", "Venue arrangements", "Equipment" and "Safety check" whenever the backend sends them, with the staff record's components (`PlanningNotes`, `VenueArrangementList`, `EquipmentOnRecord`, `SafetyOutcomeDetails`). An arrangement sent in full also shows where the venue is, what was asked for ("Gala dinner · 10 guests · Banquet"), "Why this venue: …", "Venue Staff: …", "Hold expires …" or "Hold expired …", and each flag under "Needs an alternative venue" or "Flagged after a change to the event". A reviewed line shows "Reviewed by …" and, when something is short, how many ("Partially fulfilled, 6 provided, 4 short": the shortfall, added to AC10 on 26 Oct; nothing is drawn when the shortfall is 0). Each arrangement says what its venue offers ("Holds 200 · Facilities: projector, stage · Accessibility: step-free access, hearing loop", or "none listed"). Why a venue is unavailable is worded by one shared helper (`unavailabilityText`), which the coordinator's alternative-venue notice now uses too. The confirmed-only "Venues" and "Safety check" sections and the organiser's equipment table are gone. *Default to review: the wording.*
9. **The mock backend does the same** (CONVENTIONS §14): the record mock reads each section from the mock that keeps it (the booking mock's arrangements, the equipment mocks' lines and reservations, the edits mock's notes, the history store's current check), and the history mock no longer filters what an organiser reads.
10. **Still off-limits:** other organisations' events (`404`, ADR-0020), another user's drafts (ADR-0024) the venue availability calendar (SCRUM-26) and venue search; the venue details on the record don't open the venue catalogue to them. The registrant list was already the organiser's (SCRUM-55) and stays with them and the assigned coordinator.
11. **The coordinator's record is unchanged:** they work on the arrangements, the equipment and the check from their own pages.

## Consequences
- An organiser can follow every arrangement, outcome and check on their event without asking the coordinator, at every stage.
- The notes Technical Support and Venue Staff write, the Safety Officer's checklist and comments, and the reviewers' names now reach the organiser. Staff write them knowing that.
- The staff record still lists only the arrangements that count and sends each line without its reviewer; ADR-0105, stacked on this change, moves it onto the same pieces.
- Opening the organiser's record reads the arrangements, reservations, equipment types and the newest safety entry.

## Revisit when
- The Product Owner wants any part of the record kept from organisers again.
- A change request (SCRUM-56, SCRUM-57) adds a new section to the record: the organiser's record should carry it too.
