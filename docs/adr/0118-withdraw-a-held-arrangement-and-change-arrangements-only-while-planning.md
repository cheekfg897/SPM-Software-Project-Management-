# ADR-0118: Withdrawing an On Hold arrangement releases its hold, cancel-all cancels approved bookings too, and an event's arrangements change only while it is Approved or in Safety Review

- **Status:** Accepted. Decisions 1, 3 and 8's rule are SCRUM-93's own criteria (the Product Owner's decision of 19 Oct, AC6's "cancel all of them", and AC10 as Jira has it since 15 and 17 Oct); building decision 3 here rather than in a follow-up was the user's call (1 Nov). Reading "being planned" as Approved and Safety Review, the wording, the order of the checks, and the rest marked **Default to review** are the builder's calls for the reviewer and the team. Supersedes [ADR-0117](0117-cancel-one-approved-venue-booking.md) decision 16 (cancel-all "Unchanged").
- **Date:** 2026-10-31; decisions 3, 9, 14 and 15 updated 2026-11-01 after review of #161, once it was stacked on SCRUM-111 (#160)
- **Story:** SCRUM-93 Book several venues for one event (AC6, AC7, AC9, AC10, and the "Changing an arrangement" clarification)
- **Extends:** [ADR-0039](0039-venue-arrangements.md) (decision 4's withdrawable statuses, decision 6's cancel-all, decision 13's missing event-status guard), [ADR-0069](0069-venue-arrangement-actions-in-the-web-app.md) (the Venue requests page and its mock), [ADR-0045](0045-refuse-an-approval-that-clashes.md) (holds), [ADR-0093](0093-reject-an-event-during-planning.md) (`PLANNING_STATUSES`), [ADR-0117](0117-cancel-one-approved-venue-booking.md) (cancelling one approved booking)

## Context
SCRUM-93's description asks for two things `main` did not do:

- **"Withdrawing an On Hold arrangement releases its hold in the same way (Product Owner decision, 19 Oct; matches SCRUM-92)."** `WITHDRAWABLE_REQUEST_STATUSES` held pending, conflicting, rejected and expired, so withdrawing a held arrangement was a `409`, and cancel-all was refused for the whole event while one was held. A held venue went back to the pool only when Venue Staff rejected the hold or it ran out.
- **AC10:** "The coordinator can withdraw, change or cancel an arrangement, or cancel all of an event's arrangements, only while the event is being planned, that is before it is Confirmed. ... It is also refused on a Completed, Cancelled or Rejected event. The refusal message names the event's status." The "Changed after review" note adds: "make sure withdraw, change and cancel-all all check the event's status". None of them did: ADR-0039 decision 13 listed "there is no event-status guard on withdraw, change or cancel-all" as a default to review.

SCRUM-111 (cancel one approved booking, ADR-0117) says in its AC4 that "a pending or held one is withdrawn instead", which is only true once the first point lands. Review of #161 (decker757, 31 Oct) found a third gap: cancel-all still refused whenever the event had an approved booking, which by Safety Review every active arrangement is, so AC6's and AC10's "cancel all of an event's arrangements" never worked on an event far enough along to matter. #161 was then stacked on SCRUM-111's branch (#160) to build it with SCRUM-111's cancel.

## Decision

### Withdrawing an On Hold arrangement
1. **`on_hold` is withdrawable, and withdrawing it releases its hold.** `ON_HOLD` joins `WITHDRAWABLE_REQUEST_STATUSES`. The hold's tentative `Booking` becomes `cancelled` in the same commit as the withdrawal and its `venue_arrangement_withdrawn` history entry, so the venue is free for that period at once (the calendar drops the hold, and another event's request for the period can be approved). The cancel is SCRUM-111's `_free_venue`, which rejecting a hold (SCRUM-75 AC7), releasing and cancelling also use, so the rule lives in one place (a `_cancel_hold` helper of this ADR's first version was folded into it). (Agreed: the Product Owner's decision of 19 Oct.)
2. **The hold jobs leave it alone.** `expire_holds` and `send_hold_expiry_warnings` read only requests still On Hold, so a withdrawn hold is neither expired nor warned of; tests pin both. The cancelled booking keeps its expiry, and the arrangement stops showing one (`hold_expires_at` is shown only for On Hold and Expired, SCRUM-75 AC9). **Default to review:** a warning already sent before the withdrawal is not followed by a "never mind" notice.
3. **Cancel-all retires every active arrangement, approved bookings included** (AC6, AC10). It takes a body with a `reason`, required as SCRUM-111's cancel requires one (`CancelBookingSchema`). An approved booking is cancelled as SCRUM-111 cancels one (`_cancel_and_record`: booking cancelled, arrangement Cancelled, a `venue_booking_cancelled` history entry with the reason); every other arrangement is withdrawn, a held one's hold released. All in one commit. This supersedes ADR-0117 decision 16 and ADR-0039 decision 6's refusal. Decision 15 has the details.
4. **Rejecting an event during planning still releases a held arrangement rather than withdrawing it.** `release_event_arrangements` now asks whether the arrangement holds its venue (`VENUE_HOLDING_REQUEST_STATUSES`: approved and On Hold) instead of whether it is withdrawable, so its behaviour is unchanged: a held one becomes Released, its hold cancelled, and Venue Staff are told the venue is free (ADR-0093, ADR-0103).
5. **Only an On Hold arrangement's hold is cancelled.** An Expired one's lapsed hold already frees the venue and is left as the history of when it ran out (ADR-0052). Other statuses have no hold linked to them. **Default to review:** the withdrawal does not go looking for a booking by period, as `_booking_made_by` does for an approved one, because for a conflicting arrangement that could find another arrangement's booking at the same venue and period.
6. **Nobody is told.** As for every withdrawal (ADR-0039 decision 13, ADR-0055 decision 6), no notice goes to Venue Staff, who placed the hold; it drops out of their queue. **Default to review.**
7. **The refusal now lists "held":** "Only a pending, conflicting, held, rejected or expired arrangement can be withdrawn; this one is approved." **Default to review:** the wording ("held" reads better than "on hold" in the list); the status at the end is the API's value, as before.

### AC10: the event's status
8. **"Being planned" means Approved or in Safety Review,** the events module's `PLANNING_STATUSES` (ADR-0093). Any other status refuses withdraw, change and cancel-all. Why these two:
   - there is no Planning status: planning happens while the event is Approved, and an event "returns to planning" by returning to Approved (SCRUM-22, ADR-0048, SCRUM-93's status clarification);
   - Safety Review is before Confirmed, and SCRUM-92 lets a replacement be raised there (ADR-0053), which the coordinator must be able to withdraw;
   - AC10 refuses Confirmed (changes then go through change review, SCRUM-56/57), Completed, Cancelled and Rejected;
   - an event with arrangements is never Submitted, Under Review or Awaiting Clarification on `main`: a request can only be raised for an Approved event (SCRUM-42), and the event's decision is locked once one exists (SCRUM-31 AC7, ADR-0084). (The brief for this fix said arrangements "can exist from Submitted onwards"; the code does not allow that.) **Default to review:** those statuses are refused too rather than allowed, matching the edit window the Product Owner set for SCRUM-110 on 29 Oct (ADR-0116: Approved and Safety Review) and SCRUM-111's cancel (ADR-0117).
9. **One check in the events module:** `get_event_for_arrangement_change` finds the coordinator's own event (`404` as before, ADR-0019) and refuses one that isn't being planned with `409 conflict`: "Only an approved event or one in Safety Review can have its venue arrangements withdrawn, changed or cancelled; this one is confirmed." The status is the API's value (`confirmed`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `rejected`, `submitted`, ...), as `get_approved_event_for_coordinator` and `get_event_for_replacement_booking` name it. The venue module calls it from all three services, so neither module imports the other's models. Cancelling one booking (SCRUM-111) calls it too: its own `get_event_for_booking_cancellation` and message ("...can have a venue booking cancelled...") were folded into this one once both were on one base, so AC10 is one rule with one message (decker757's should 4). **Default to review:** one message for every action rather than one each.
10. **The order of the checks.** Withdraw: the reference and the coordinator (`404`), the event's status (`409`), then the arrangement (`404`) and its status (`409`), so an unknown arrangement id on a Confirmed event is refused for the event. Cancel-all: the reference and the coordinator, the event's status, then the body (`400`). A change keeps its body first (`400`, which the web app's contract test pins "before looking for the event"), then the same order. Nothing is written on a refusal. **Default to review.**
11. **A draft is still never found** (`404`, ADR-0024), and **listing an event's arrangements stays allowed at any status**: it changes nothing.

### The web app and the mock
12. **The Venue requests page is unchanged.** It already offers Withdraw on every arrangement that isn't retired, held ones included, and shows any refusal in the backend's words (ADR-0069 decisions 2 and 7). **Default to review:** it does not hide the actions on a Confirmed event; the backend's message explains the refusal.
13. **The booking mock follows** (CONVENTIONS §14): `on_hold` is withdrawable, withdrawing or cancelling all clears the hold's expiry (now in `moveTo`, for every status without a hold, which also fixes a hold released by rejecting the event keeping its expiry in the mock), `releaseEventArrangements` releases a held arrangement, and withdraw, change and cancel-all go through `eventToChange`, which answers the backend's `404` and AC10 `409` in the backend's order; SCRUM-111's cancel uses it too, and the mock declares `PLANNING_EVENT_STATUSES` once (decker757's should 5). The contract tests check both changes against the real backend: a held request withdrawn and its venue free (`holds.int.test.ts`), and the refusals on a Confirmed and a Rejected event, after a Safety Review event let them through (`booking.int.test.ts`, replacing its `it.todo`).

### Racing a decision
14. **Withdraw, change and cancel-all read the arrangement under a row lock,** as Venue Staff's decisions do (ADR-0045): `_arrangement_of_event` reads it with `FOR UPDATE` and `populate_existing` (the shared `_read_locked`, which `_locked_request` now uses too), then checks it belongs to the event. SCRUM-111's cancel uses it as well (its own `_locked_arrangement_of_event` was folded in). Cancel-all locks and re-reads each active arrangement, earliest period first, before withdrawing or cancelling it, so a booking Venue Staff approved meanwhile is cancelled rather than withdrawn. Without it, Venue Staff approving a hold (or a Pending request) while the coordinator withdrew it would end with the request Withdrawn and its booking cancelled after both sides were told "approved", or, for Pending, a live booking with no arrangement behind it (decker757, review of #161). With it the second action waits for the first and is refused for the status the first saved. SQLite ignores `FOR UPDATE`, so the unit tests show the statement sent and, with a decision written beneath the session's cached copy, that the action sees it and refuses (`tests/venue/test_venue_arrangement_locks.py`).

### Cancelling all, approved bookings included
15. **How cancel-all treats a booking** (decision 3, decker757's must 1):
    - **The reason is required on every cancel-all,** even for an event with no approved booking, where it is recorded nowhere. **Default to review:** one rule the web app can state, rather than a body that is required only sometimes.
    - **The reason goes on each booking cancelled,** in its `venue_booking_cancelled` entry, as SCRUM-111 AC5 records it. A withdrawn arrangement keeps its usual `venue_arrangement_withdrawn` entry, which has no reason. **Default to review.**
    - **SCRUM-111 AC2's Safety Review rules apply once for the whole action,** through `return_to_planning_after_cancellation`, whenever anything was retired: a Safety Officer's pass no longer counts (one `safety_approval_withdrawn` entry, naming the first arrangement retired, earliest period first), and an event left with no venue, which cancel-all always leaves, goes back to Approved (SCRUM-97 AC3). **Default to review:** naming the first arrangement, and applying it when only unapproved requests were withdrawn too.
    - **Who is told,** after the commit, as SCRUM-111 tells for one booking (`_tell_of_cancellations`, which cancel-one now shares): every Venue Staff user, and for an event that was in Safety Review every Safety Officer, get one notice per booking cancelled, naming its venue and period (SCRUM-111 AC2, AC3). Nobody is told of a withdrawal (decision 6). **Default to review:** one notice per booking rather than one for the whole action.
    - **The web app** asks for the reason in the "Cancel all venue requests" form, as the cancel-one form does: the reason is sent only when typed, so the backend's message shows under the box, and the button reads "Cancel them all". The mock and the contract tests follow.

## Consequences
- A coordinator can drop a held venue at once instead of waiting for the hold to run out or asking Venue Staff to reject it, and cancel-all works on any event being planned, including one in Safety Review where every arrangement is an approved booking.
- SCRUM-111 AC4's "a pending or held one is withdrawn instead" is now true, and SCRUM-111's cancel shares this ADR's planning check, refusal message and locked lookup (decisions 9 and 14).
- #161 is stacked on SCRUM-111's branch (#160) and merges after it.
- A Confirmed event's arrangements can't be withdrawn, changed or cancelled until change review (SCRUM-56/57) exists; that is AC10's intent.
- The unit tests are `tests/venue/test_venue_arrangements.py` (On Hold), `tests/venue/test_venue_arrangement_event_status.py` (AC10, every event status built from the enum), `tests/venue/test_venue_arrangement_locks.py` (decision 14), `tests/venue/test_cancel_all_arrangements.py` (decision 15) and, for AC9, `test_a_change_cannot_move_the_venue_or_the_period`.

## Revisit when
- SCRUM-56/57 (change review) are built: a Confirmed event's arrangement changes go through them.
- The team wants Venue Staff told when a hold they placed is withdrawn (decision 6), one message per action (decision 9), or one notice for a whole cancel-all (decision 15).
