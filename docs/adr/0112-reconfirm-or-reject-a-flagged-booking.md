# ADR-0112: Venue Staff reconfirm a flagged booking for a period moved by the event's start, or reject it with a reason

- **Status:** Accepted for what SCRUM-59's criteria and the Product Owner's answers of 27 Oct settle (AC1-AC11: raising the occupancy, a reason for a venue too small, moving an undecided request without approving it); decisions 1-5 were made by the lead before the build and decisions 6-16 while building, all defaults to review; decision 17 was raised in review and agreed with the Product Owner (31 Oct)
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Story:** SCRUM-59 Reconfirm or reject a booking flagged after a change
- **Extends:** [ADR-0094](0094-reassess-each-venue-arrangement-after-a-significant-change.md) (the flags, and its "Revisit when SCRUM-59 lands"), [ADR-0045](0045-refuse-an-approval-that-clashes.md) (the clash rule and the venue lock), [ADR-0035](0035-venue-booking-requests.md) (Venue Staff see every booking), [ADR-0015](0015-in-app-notifications.md)

## Context
SCRUM-33 AC6 flags each live venue arrangement a significant change affects (ADR-0094), and SCRUM-58 will flag the same way for an approved change request. The flag holds the event back from Safety Review and confirmation (SCRUM-97, SCRUM-49). Until now the only ways to clear one were a replacement, a withdrawal or a rejection through the queue. SCRUM-59 gives Venue Staff the two decisions the flag asks for:

- **Reconfirm** (AC2-AC4, AC10, AC11): the booking moves to the new period, pre-filled by "moving the booking by the same amount the event's start moved", within the event's new start and end, refused on a conflict, with a reason when the venue is too small for the occupancy. A booking flagged because attendance rose is raised to the new attendance. An undecided request only moves.
- **Reject** (AC5): with a reason, freeing the venue; the booking shows as Rejected and holds the event back until the coordinator withdraws or replaces it.

The criteria leave open how "the amount the start moved" is measured, where the flagged bookings appear, what the reason is stored as, and what the coordinator is told.

## Decision
### Agreed with the lead before the build (defaults to review)
1. **The pre-filled period moves the booking by the total amount the event's start moved since the booking's period was last set**, however many edits that took. The events module reads it back from the event's history (`get_event_when_arranged`): the last `venue_booking_requested` or `venue_booking_reconfirmed` entry naming the arrangement marks when its period was set, and each field's first `field_changed` entry after that gives its value from before. No column is stored. "Since it was approved" in the lead's wording is read as "since its period was set": approving books the period the request already had, so a request approved while flagged would otherwise lose the move made before its approval. A booking with no such entry (saved before SCRUM-42 wrote history) counts every change. The move is worked out in UTC, which is the same as in business time because Singapore has no daylight saving.
2. **Venue Staff may reject any flagged booking**, with a reason, not only one that is no longer suitable.
3. SCRUM-99's arrangement flag will be a `requires_reconfirmation` flag reading "Flagged by the Safety Officer: <reason>", and this story's reconfirm and reject clear it (ADR-0113). Reconfirming one the change didn't move keeps its period (AC2).
4. **No alternative venue is suggested** on a reject (SCRUM-70 is out of scope).
5. **A tentative hold left on the old period is out of scope** (SCRUM-109).

### Made while building (defaults to review)
6. **Where they appear (AC1).** `GET /api/venues/booking-requests/flagged` lists every Pending, Conflicting or Approved arrangement with an open change flag, earliest event first, as Venue Staff's queue orders requests. Each item is `{"booking": <the booking as every list shows it, flags and reasons included>, "reconfirm_with": {"starts_at", "ends_at", "expected_occupancy", "period_adjustable"}}` (decision 17). One that also needs an alternative venue is left out, and reconfirming or rejecting it is refused (`409`), since AC1 says that flag clears only under SCRUM-92. The web app shows them in a "Flagged after a change" section of the Venue Staff booking page, between the queue and the approved bookings. A flagged Pending request is in the queue too, since it still waits for a decision.
7. **Reconfirm** is `POST /api/venues/booking-requests/<id>/reconfirm` with `starts_at`, `ends_at` (both required: the page sends the pre-filled period, adjusted or not) and an optional `reason`. **Reject** is `POST .../<id>/reject` with `reason`. Both take the booking under a row lock, as a decision does.
8. **The checks, in order:** the booking is flagged (409), the body (400), no replacement for it is waiting for a decision (409, AC6; Pending, Conflicting or On Hold), the booking's own period when the event's date and times didn't change (400, decision 17), the period within the event's start and end (400 on `starts_at` or `ends_at`, naming the event's times), inside the venue's hours (400, the SCRUM-42 message), a reason if the venue's capacity is below the occupancy (400 on `reason`), then with the venue's setup and turnaround: another booking or a live hold (409 naming the booking and the period it holds), a block (409 naming its period and category), another of the event's live arrangements at the venue (409 naming it). The booking's own booking is left out of the clash check. The venue is read under its lock while the period is checked (ADR-0045).
9. **The same checks apply to an undecided request** (AC11). It moves into a period it could later be approved for; moving it into a clash would leave it only to be rejected.
10. **The reason** is trimmed, and a blank one counts as missing (AC4). On reconfirm it is kept as Venue Staff's reason (`decision_reason`), which the event record and the coordinator's list already show beside the booking, and goes into the notice; a reconfirmation without one keeps the earlier reason. The coordinator's own justification (SCRUM-42) is left as they wrote it.
11. **Occupancy (AC10).** A booking whose occupancy covered the attendance when its period was set, and which the attendance has since outgrown, is reconfirmed for the attendance as it is now, using the reassessment's own test (`_outgrown`). A smaller booking keeps its occupancy.
12. **What reconfirming does.** The booking and its venue booking move, the occupancy is set, the change flags are deleted, preparation details are kept (AC9), and the event's other bookings are untouched (AC8). A Conflicting request moved clear of every booking becomes Pending again; an approved one marks the Pending requests its new period clashes with as Conflicting, as an approval does (SCRUM-44). A `venue_booking_reconfirmed` history entry records the period and occupancy before and after, the reason, the status and the flags cleared, in the same commit; it is also what the next reconfirmation measures from (decision 1).
13. **What rejecting does.** The booking becomes Rejected with the reason, an approved one's venue booking is cancelled, and the flags stay as the record of why (ADR-0094 decision 2). The `venue_booking_rejected` entry and notice are reused. The event stays Approved.
14. **Who is told.** The event's current coordinator (not whoever raised the request): a new `venue_booking_reconfirmed` notice, "Venue Staff reconfirmed a venue booking for your event after a change.", whose detail names the arrangement, the venue and the new period ("... is reconfirmed for 2026-11-02 09:00 to 2026-11-02 12:00."), or for an undecided request that it "moved to ... and waits for Venue Staff's decision", then any raised occupancy and the reason; or the existing `venue_booking_rejected` notice with the reason. Sent after the commit; one that can't be saved loses only itself.
15. **Migration `0049_add_venue_booking_reconfirmed`** adds the value to `notification_kind` and `event_history_entry_kind`; its downgrade deletes the rows and rebuilds both types. It revises #149's `0048_create_revoked_tokens`.
16. **The mock** lists, reconfirms and rejects as the backend does, keeps what changed under each booking as edits arrive (the backend reads it from the history), and leaves out venue hours, blocks and setup and turnaround, as its approval already does. The planning gates' own arrangements (`eventPlanning`) are not cleared by it: they are a separate store from the booking mock's, as ADR-0107 decision 10 notes.
17. **The period stays unless the event's date or times changed (AC2).** Venue Staff may adjust the pre-filled period only when the event's start or end has moved since the booking's period was set, worked out as decision 1 works out the move; AC2 says a change that didn't touch the date or time keeps the period, and the clarification lets Venue Staff adjust it only to apply the coordinator's change (customer, Week 4, A75). After a change to attendance or requirements, or a Safety Officer's flag (decision 3), reconfirming keeps the booking's own period, and any other is refused (400 on `starts_at` or `ends_at`, whichever changed, naming the period it keeps). An end that moved on its own counts as a change, since an earlier end can leave the booking running past the event, which only a new period settles; edits that move the event and back again don't. `reconfirm_with.period_adjustable` says which applies, and the page shows a period it can't adjust as read-only. Raised in review and agreed with the Product Owner (31 Oct), the end moved on its own included.

## Consequences
- A change no longer has to be undone by a replacement: Venue Staff settle each flagged booking where they decide requests, and the event can go on to Safety Review once each is settled.
- SCRUM-58's flags, made through the same `flag_arrangements_after_change`, are settled here unchanged, as long as an approved change request also writes its edits to the history as `field_changed` entries: the pre-filled move, the occupancy raise and whether the period may be adjusted are all read back from them (decisions 1, 11, 17), and without them each comes out as no change.
- Approving a flagged Pending request through the queue is not refused: it stays flagged until reconfirmed, and reconfirming then moves its booking.
- Tests: `tests/venue/test_flagged_bookings.py`, `test_reconfirm_flagged_booking.py`, `test_reject_flagged_booking.py`, `test_flagged_booking_routes.py`, `test_venue_booking_reconfirmed_migration.py` (marker `scrum_59`); the mock's `bookingFlagged.test.ts`; `BookingDecisionsPage.flagged.test.tsx`; `flaggedBookings.int.test.ts` against the backend.

## Revisit when
- The team wants the period measured from the approval rather than from when it was set (decision 1), or the business timezone gains daylight saving.
- The team wants approving a flagged request through the queue refused until it is reconfirmed.
- SCRUM-70 suggests an alternative venue on a reject, or SCRUM-109 releases a hold left on the old period.
