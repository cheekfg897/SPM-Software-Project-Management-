# ADR-0127: The coordinator approves or rejects a change request

- **Status:** Accepted; the decision, the history, the reason to reject, the notices, the stale refusal and holding the event's decisions are SCRUM-57's criteria; the rest are defaults to review
- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Story:** SCRUM-57 Review and decide a change request
- **Extends:** [ADR-0126](0126-request-a-change-after-submitting.md) (raising and withdrawing a change request), [ADR-0025](0025-event-edits-significant-changes-and-history.md) (field changes in the history), [ADR-0114](0114-reassess-equipment-reservations-after-a-change.md) (equipment after a date or time change), [ADR-0015](0015-in-app-notifications.md), [ADR-0105](0105-reviewer-and-every-arrangement-on-the-staff-record.md)

## Context
SCRUM-56 lets the Organiser of a submitted event ask for a change to its details: one Pending change request per event, keeping what each field held when it was raised. SCRUM-57 has the event's coordinator decide it. Approving applies the change and records it in the history; rejecting needs a reason and changes nothing. The Organiser is told either way, and registered Attendees are told when an approved change moves the date or time. A request is decided once. One that a later edit has overtaken is refused, naming the fields, and stays Pending. While one is Pending, the event itself can't be approved, rejected or confirmed. The criteria leave open who exactly decides, what a stale request is, which statuses an approval can apply in, and how the event page shows all of this.

## Decision
1. **`POST /api/events/requests/<reference>/change-requests/<id>/decide`** takes `{"outcome": "approved" | "rejected", "reason"}`, the body SCRUM-31's `/decide` takes, and answers `200` with the change request as the record shows it. Only the event's assigned coordinator may decide; anyone else's event is a `404`, as for every coordinator action (ADR-0024). Rejecting without a reason is a `400` on `reason` (AC3).
2. **The event's row is locked first** (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), as raising and withdrawing do (ADR-0126). Two decisions, or a decision and a withdrawal, take turns: the first wins and the second sees a request no longer Pending, a `409 change_request_not_pending` naming its status (AC5, technical note).
3. **Approving checks, then applies.** The event must still be in a status a change can be raised in, Confirmed included, though a Confirmed event can't be edited directly (ADR-0101). An event that is Completed, Cancelled or Rejected ran, or won't run, as it was, so approving is a `409 change_request_not_allowed` that says to reject instead. The request can still be rejected.
4. **A request is stale when a field it changes now holds something other than the value kept when it was raised.** Approving one is a `409 change_request_stale` that names each such field with its value then and now ("Proposed date: 2026-10-15 when the change was requested, 2026-10-18 now."), in the request's order. The request stays Pending (AC8). The message says to reject it so the Organiser can raise a new one. A field changed and changed back is not stale, and a change to a field the request doesn't touch doesn't make it stale.
5. **The requested values are checked as an edit checks them**, against the event as it is now and today's date: an approval never saves an event that ends before it starts or starts in the past. A value that no longer fits is a `400` on its field.
6. **Each applied change is written to the history as the coordinator's `field_changed` entry**, in the order a direct edit writes them (ADR-0025). A change to the date moves the end date with it, as an edit does. The decision itself is recorded on the change request (status, `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `decision_reason`), not as a separate history entry. Two database checks keep a decided request with who and when, and a rejection with its reason (migration 0055).
7. **An approved change reaches the equipment as a direct edit does.** A change to the date or time hands the equipment back to Technical Support in the decision's commit (`reassess_equipment_after_timing_change`, SCRUM-60, ADR-0114): every reservation is marked for reassessment and stays held for its old period, and a line recorded unavailable or partially fulfilled is reopened. An event in Safety Review left with either goes back to Approved (ADR-0107); a Confirmed one stays Confirmed. Once saved, Technical Support are told with `significant_change` when a changed field could affect the equipment and the event has some, as after an edit (ADR-0025). Re-checking the venue bookings against the new details, and withdrawing a safety approval, are SCRUM-58's. The decision leaves one marked place for them, before the commit, where a direct edit flags its arrangements and withdraws the approval.
8. **The Organiser is told** with `change_request_approved` or `change_request_rejected`, with the coordinator's reason as the detail, after the decision commits (AC4, ADR-0015).
9. **Every Attendee holding a place is told** with a new `event_rescheduled` kind when an approved change moves the date, end date, start time or end time, with the event's new period as the detail (AC7). Who holds a place is the registration module's answer (`list_registered_attendee_ids`). Someone who withdrew isn't told.
10. **While a change request is Pending, the event's own decisions wait.** SCRUM-31's approve and reject, SCRUM-49's rejection during planning and its confirmation lock the row and are refused with `409 change_request_pending`: "The organiser has a change request waiting for your decision. Decide the change request first." (AC9). A withdrawn or decided request doesn't hold them, and edits and clarification requests aren't held.
11. **The record shows the decision.** Each change request on the record carries `decided_by` (by name), `decided_at` and `decision_reason`, and its status shows Approved or Rejected (AC6). The staff record (ADR-0095) now carries `change_requests` too.
12. **The coordinator reviews the request in one panel on the event page** (AC1): each requested field beside its current value, every venue arrangement from the record, read-only, with the list the page already uses, and the equipment reservations read from the existing `GET /api/equipment/events/<reference>/reservations`. The coordinator's record stays without reservations (ADR-0105).

## Consequences
- Migration 0055 adds the decision's columns and checks, and the three notification kinds.
- A coordinator who edits a field after the Organiser asked to change it has to reject the request; the Organiser raises a new one against the edited event.
- Until SCRUM-58, approving a change of date or attendance leaves the venue bookings as they were, unflagged, and a safety approval in place.

## Revisit when
- SCRUM-58 reassesses the venue bookings after an approved change.
- The Product Owner wants approvals of over events, or a history entry for the decision itself.
