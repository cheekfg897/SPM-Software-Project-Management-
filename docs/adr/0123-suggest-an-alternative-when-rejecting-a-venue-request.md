# ADR-0123: Venue Staff may suggest one alternative venue, period or both when they reject a booking request

- **Status:** Accepted. One suggestion, given only while rejecting, an active venue, no check of the period until a request is raised for it, an editable pre-fill, no automatic withdrawal, one notice, the same visibility as the reason, and a replacement's locked period are the Product Owner's decisions (SCRUM-70, 26 Oct). The storage, the wording of the notice and the screens, the checks' order and messages, and carrying a rejected replacement's link into the new request are the team's defaults to review.
- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Story:** SCRUM-70 Suggest an alternative when rejecting a booking request
- **Extends:** [ADR-0042](0042-venue-booking-decisions.md) (the decision payload, decision 6; the reason on the request, decision 15), [ADR-0121](0121-venue-decision-notices-go-to-the-current-coordinator.md) (who is told), [ADR-0108](0108-organiser-notice-locked-replacement-period-and-available-again-wording.md) (a replacement's locked period)

## Context
The customer's briefing lets Venue Staff "approve it, reject it, or suggest another venue or arrangement" (Step 7), but SCRUM-43 only approves or rejects with a reason. The customer answered that a suggestion is "Optional, this is not considered as core requirement" (Week 4, A44), and that a resubmission counts as a new booking for audit (FAQ). On 26 Oct the Product Owner wrote the team's defaults into SCRUM-70's criteria: one alternative venue, one alternative period, or both, only while rejecting; on a replacement for a booking flagged Needs Alternative Venue (SCRUM-92) only a venue, because the replacement's period is fixed; the venue must be active and the period is not checked until the coordinator raises a request for it (SCRUM-42); the suggestion is shown wherever the reason is, the Event Organiser included; the coordinator raises a new request pre-filled from it and can change it, and the rejected one is not withdrawn (SCRUM-93); the suggestion holds nothing; and the event's current coordinator is told once, by the rejection notice (SCRUM-115).

Main already had "alternative venue" in it, but that is SCRUM-92's flag on a booking whose venue became unavailable (`AlternativeVenueFlag`, `record_alternative_venue_needed`). Nothing stored or showed a suggestion.

## Decision
1. **A suggestion is three nullable columns on the request**: `suggested_venue_id` (a foreign key to `venues`), `suggested_starts_at` and `suggested_ends_at`, added by migration 0051. The database keeps the period whole (`ck_venue_booking_requests_suggestion_period_whole`) and ending after it starts (`..._suggestion_ends_after_start`). There is no rule tying the columns to the Rejected status, because a rejected request may later be withdrawn and its suggestion is still the record of what was offered. Keeping it only in the history entry was the alternative; every reader of the reason would then have needed a history lookup.
2. **The decision payload gains `suggested_venue_id`, `suggested_starts_at` and `suggested_ends_at`, flat and optional**, beside `outcome` and `reason`. Flat, like the request's other fields, so each refusal lands on the field the web app shows it under. A part sent as `null` counts as not sent. A time with no offset is business time (ADR-0009).
3. **The schema checks the shape; the service checks the rules**, as the reason already splits (ADR-0042 decision 6). The schema refuses a venue id that isn't a whole number from 1, half a period ("A suggested period needs both a start and an end." on the missing end), and an end at or before the start. The service then refuses, in this order: any suggestion with an approval, on each field sent ("An alternative can only be suggested when rejecting a request."); a venue that is unknown or inactive, both as "The suggested venue must be an active venue." on `suggested_venue_id`; and a period on a replacement whose arrangement needs an alternative venue, on both period fields. A replacement for an arrangement flagged only after a change to the event may be given a period, since its own period may move (ADR-0100). The suggested venue isn't compared with the venue a replacement replaces. A blocked venue is still active, so Venue Staff could suggest it, and raising that suggestion is then refused by SCRUM-92's "A replacement must be at a different venue from the one it replaces." The coordinator changes the venue and carries on, so it isn't a dead end; refusing it here would be a rule nobody has asked for (default to review).
4. **A suggestion books and holds nothing** (AC5). The rejection path is unchanged apart from storing it, so another event may book the suggested venue for the suggested period.
5. **The suggestion is everywhere the reason is** (AC3): on the booking request (`suggested_venue_id`, and the period in business time), on the record's arrangement (`suggested_venue_name` and the period), which the organiser, the coordinator and staff all read, and in the decision's history entry (`suggested_venue_id`, and the period in UTC as the request's own entry writes it), where only what was suggested is recorded. The coordinator's list names the venue by id, as it names the request's own venue; the record names it, as it names its own.
6. **The rejection notice carries the suggestion, before the reason** (AC6): "Suggested instead: Breakout room from 2026-11-03 14:00 to 2026-11-03 17:00. Reason: …", with "the same venue" or "for the same period" for a part not suggested. The reason goes last because it is Venue Staff's own words and may or may not end in a full stop. The recipient is the event's current coordinator (ADR-0121); there is no second notice and no new notification kind.
7. **The coordinator raises a new request from the suggestion** (AC4) through a link under it, which opens the request form with `?suggestion=<id>`, as a replacement's link carries its arrangement (ADR-0088). The form takes the suggested venue and period, or the rejected request's own where none was suggested, and the rejected request's purpose, occupancy and layout. Every field stays editable and SCRUM-42's checks run when it is sent. A new request is an ordinary request with no link back to the rejected one (the customer's "new booking for audit"), except that a rejected replacement's new request names the booking it replaces, so approving it still releases that booking (SCRUM-92). The rejected request stays Rejected until the coordinator withdraws it.
8. **Venue Staff give the suggestion on the decisions screen**: a "Suggest an alternative" group under the reason with a venue ID and a start and end typed in Singapore time. Fields left empty are left out. The web app does not hide the group on approval or on a replacement: the backend's message shows under the field, as for the reason.

## Consequences
- Migration 0051 revises 0050 (SCRUM-60), main's head when this was written. Other open pull requests also number migrations from 0050: #160 (SCRUM-111) claims 0051 too, with #161 and #167 (SCRUM-61's 0052) stacked on it, and #169 (SCRUM-56) adds 0053. Whichever merges later renumbers and re-points its `down_revision`. Its downgrade drops the columns, so it loses any suggestion given since; the history entries keep them.
- The venue module's decision path reads one more venue (the suggested one) and the record one more venue name per arrangement with a suggestion.
- The booking mock now looks venues up in the catalogue when a request is raised, as the backend does, so a request can be raised at a suggested venue in mock mode.
- The coordinator's list still shows venues by id, the suggested one included.

## Revisit when
- Venue Staff need to suggest more than one alternative, or a free-text arrangement rather than a venue and period.
- The coordinator's list starts naming venues, when the suggestion should too.
- A story needs to trace a new request back to the suggestion it came from.
